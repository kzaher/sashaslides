# One step per line, in order. `record.ts step <n>` captions step n; {URL} is the connector address.
START: claude.ai home page (claude.ai/new), already signed in
1. In the left sidebar, click Customize.
2. At the top of Customize, click the Connectors tab.
3. Top right, click the black + Add button.
4. In the menu, click Add custom connector.
5. Click the Name field and type: Sasha Slides
6. Click the MCP server URL field and type the connector address: {URL}
7. Click Continue.
8. Leave the detected defaults: Authentication = Sign in now, OAuth client = Register automatically. Scroll to the bottom of the dialog.
9. Click Add.
10. Click Connect. The same tab goes to the Sasha Slides sign-in, which hands you to Google.
10a. Google asks you to choose an account: click your account.
10b. Google warns 'Google hasn't verified this app' (the Sasha Slides app is in testing): click Continue.
10c. Google shows 'Sign in to Sasha Slides' with your account: click Continue.
10d. On 'Sasha Slides wants additional access to your Google Account' (Google Slides access), click the round down-arrow button to scroll to the end; Continue becomes active.
10e. Click Continue. Google returns you to Sasha Slides, which returns you to Claude.
11. Connected: the page now shows Disconnect and the 12 Sasha Slides tools (each 'Needs approval' by default).
12. In the left sidebar click New to start a chat.
13. Click the message box, type: Use the sasha-slides connector: call open_work and tell me what is waiting for me.  — then press Enter.
14. Claude asks to use 'What is waiting for me' from Sasha Slides: click Allow once (or Always allow).
15. Claude calls the tool and answers from your own Drive: five proposals, the newest with 8 candidates and 3 unresolved comments.
