# One step per line, in order. `record.ts step <n>` captions step n; {URL} is the connector address.
START: chatgpt.com home page, signed in (ChatGPT Plus)
1. In the left sidebar, click Plugins.
2. Top right, click Add.
3. In the menu, click Add custom MCP server.
4. Name: type Sasha Slides
5. Description (optional): type Candidate slides for Google Slides
6. Connection: keep Server URL selected and type the connector address: {URL}
7. Leave Authentication on OAuth (ChatGPT registers itself; no client ID or secret needed). Tick 'I understand and want to continue'.
8. Click Create as a plugin.
9. ChatGPT shows 'Connect Sasha Slides': click Continue to Sasha Slides. The same tab goes to the Sasha Slides sign-in, which hands you to Google.
9a. Google asks you to choose an account: click your account.
9b. Google warns 'Google hasn't verified this app' (the Sasha Slides app is in testing): click Continue.
9c. Google shows the sign-in to sashaslides.com with your account: click Continue.
9d. On 'sashaslides.com wants additional access to your Google Account' (Google Slides access), click the round down-arrow button to scroll to the end; Continue becomes active.
9e. Click Continue. Google returns you to Sasha Slides, which returns you to ChatGPT.
10. Signed in: Sasha Slides now appears under Installed on the Plugins page.
11. In the left sidebar click New chat.
12. Click the message box and type @Sasha; pick Sasha Slides from the list that appears.
13. After the Sasha Slides chip, type: call open_work and tell me what is waiting for me. — then press Enter.
14. ChatGPT calls Sasha Slides open_work and answers from your own Drive: the proposals waiting for you, or that there are none yet.
