Reliability Issues

Q3 incident review — four recurring themes

  1. 1
    Retry storms
    Uncapped client retries amplified a 500ms blip into a 12-minute outage.
  2. 2
    Silent queue backlog
    Worker lag had no alert until the dead-letter queue crossed 40k messages.
  3. 3
    Config drift
    Staging and prod diverged on timeout values after a manual hotfix.
  4. 4
    Cold cache stampede
    Simultaneous cache expiry hammered the primary DB at peak traffic.
